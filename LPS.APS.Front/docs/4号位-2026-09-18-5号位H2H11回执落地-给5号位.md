**5号位 修复内容**（@see `lps/LPS.APS.BusinessRules/Repositories/OrderQueryRepository.cs` L207-218）：

```sql
-- 改前（引用不存在的值）
SUM(CASE WHEN o.DelayStatus = 'DELAYED' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS DelayedCount

-- 改后（冻结值域 — 字段文档 v5.1.7 L2546/L3350/L3481）
SUM(CASE WHEN o.DelayStatus IN ('FIRST_DELAY', 'REPEATED_DELAY') THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS DelayedCount
```

- L210 OnTimeCount：`DelayStatus IS NULL OR = 'ON_TIME'` ✅ 与字段文档 `ON_TIME` 值域吻合
